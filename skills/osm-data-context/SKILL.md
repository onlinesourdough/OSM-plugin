# OSM:data-context

Own the idempotent engineering of one customer-owned Notion Data & Context home.
The [Context Architecture](references/architecture.md) is the one contract for
its root, single definition, core Knowledge, Memory and Skills roles, their
properties and useful views, access and readiness; "guide" in these references
means the root entry. OSM supplies the method, never company data. A skill guides
the running agent; it is not a background sync.

OSM is an AI-assisted method and framework for solo founders and small teams.
Data and context are its two foundation parts, and skills sit inside context.
Notion is where that foundation lives. External systems, local files and
repositories remain valid sources or working artifacts; they are not alternate
context homes, and nothing here sets up a folder, vault or other provider home.

## Engineer the home

- Reuse suitable existing or native stores by their actual ID, create only what is
  missing, and never duplicate a home, store, profile or skill by title. A replay
  finds the same objects and writes nothing.
- Never create Content. Content is a separate, tailored method outside OSM. A
  Content store the owner already selected stays readable and maintainable
  through Manage Data & Context without any Content module installed.
- The definition describes actual roles, IDs, scope, source/destination/authority
  mappings and operational bindings. Host paths are host-specific execution
  bindings, not business data locations. Knowledge owns the business's goals,
  audience, positioning, offers, voice, channels and working preferences.
- Discover the actual types and metadata of existing stores before mapping
  anything onto them, and keep the owner's local field mappings as small owner
  configuration. The [schema contract](references/notion/schema-and-templates.md)
  and the architecture's record lifecycle carry the rest.
- Follow [Notion setup](references/notion/setup.md) and its
  [acceptance](references/notion/acceptance.md) for a new or existing workspace,
  the [root template](assets/data-and-context-root.md) for the entry, and the
  [Home projection procedure](references/panel-links.md) for the panel.
  [Migration](references/migration.md) covers a root change.
- The owner's recorded [write policy](references/architecture.md#home-write-policy)
  decides what upkeep may write; read it, never hardcode it, and never infer a
  grant. A guide cannot grant permissions by its content, except the root's
  recorded `write_policy`.
- Keep Skills a first-class role. Where a personal or team method is created,
  changed or placed, Manage Skills owns that lifecycle; this skill only
  provides the role and its properties.

Setup leads onboarding and calls this skill for the engineering step; ongoing
retrieval, intake and curation belong to Manage Data & Context. A general
question or an independent repository task needs no owner-context preload. Do
not fall back to another home when Notion is unavailable: hold dependent changes
and report the exact gap. Installation alone neither connects nor configures a
home, and an installed connector alone never selects a home or authorizes
migration.

At completion, verify the affected records and routes and keep setup, successful
retrieval, actual skill use and future operation separate. Report bounded
observations, unsaved work and remaining capability gaps.
