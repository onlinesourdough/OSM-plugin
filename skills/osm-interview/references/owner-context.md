# First owner context

Use only when the selected interview needs basic owner context that is not
already available. Reuse an established focus; an existing owner home does not
need another foundation interview.

Invite an optional website, profile, document or short description of the
person's work in their language. No public presence or connector is required.
Read supplied, authorized relevant sources, confirm identity and distinguish
public positioning from internal facts. Failed access leaves a gap that an
owner explanation can fill; it never licenses invented facts.

For founders and business leaders, Offer (what, for whom, result and
responsibility), Operations (delivery, capacity and dependencies) and Demand
(how suitable customers find, trust and choose the business) can reveal useful
connections. They are lenses, not compulsory categories or three problems to
solve. Follow a concrete recent example of the experienced difficulty before
proposing a system. Avoid demanding an arbitrary target or deadline.

When the location of needed knowledge is unclear, ask where that particular
work happens. Use existing authorized access. New connections and registration
belong to [Setup](../../osm-setup/SKILL.md) only when needed and authorized;
otherwise continue from available context. Source routes and unique local facts
use Maintain Context's [curation](../../osm-manage-data-context/references/curation.md).
Understanding can itself be the small useful result.
