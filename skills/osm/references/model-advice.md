# Model advice, on request

Give concise model and reasoning-effort advice only when asked, or when a
delegated route cannot proceed without one. Humans keep the model choice. Advice
is a recommendation: it grants no authority to switch a model, provider or
account, to spend, or to change configuration.

- Respect an explicit model or effort. A worker keeps the model and effort it
  was explicitly given. If that route is unavailable, report it and stop or
  hand back; never substitute another model, account or billing route silently.
- Do not invent equivalent reasoning tiers across providers or controls that
  are not exposed. Defaults do not prove the active model, and catalogs prove
  neither access nor authority.
- Reuse current runtime evidence. Inspect models, efforts, tools and controls
  only for an unresolved suitability gap, and separate active settings,
  new-task defaults and worker controls.

## Assess what judgment remains

Take a second look at the accepted Spec and specialist outputs: what is settled,
overlooked or still needs judgment? Give a reasoned recommendation, not a score,
fixed threshold or mandatory separate reviewer. Unsettled choices, consequences,
context dependence and proof may still require strong reasoning (visual
direction, narrative, voice, architecture, interacting constraints). Approved
components, templates or formatting may suit a cheaper route. Phase, domain or
volume alone selects no model. READY/PASS does not make Build or Ship mechanical.

Choose sufficient capability, then total task cost: context, reasoning, tools,
transfer, coordination, review and retries. Separate tokens, money and time and
follow the owner's priorities. Cheaper inference alone proves no net gain. Use
[measurement](model-measurement.md) for a performance claim or an authorized
comparison; a pilot proves no universal default. For a material evidence gap,
make a bounded lookup of official capability, reasoning and price documentation,
then relevant public measurements, and record date, version, effort, harness,
task family and metric. Missing or conflicting evidence lowers confidence
without blocking a sufficient route.

## Return and apply

Return the remaining judgment, model and effort, evidence, reason, how to apply
it, and any gaps in the existing task contract. Low remaining judgment permits a
cheaper candidate, not automatic delegation. A material capability or cost gap
reopens the shared [task-result decision](lifecycle.md).
[Orchestrate workers](../../osm-orchestrate-workers/SKILL.md) uses advice only
for already selected delegated execution.

Apply an authorized, exposed in-session switch when appropriate. If only the user
can switch, give the context and the exact selection, and require native readback
before claiming it applied. A selected handoff uses [continuation](continuation.md).

Keep inventories and benchmark tables in the task, never in owner memory,
templates, synced configuration or this package. A chosen route, reason and date
may be historical evidence. Persist a default only for an authorized native
setup change, preserving unrelated settings and verifying readback. Promise no
automatic future tracking, and reassess only on a changed runtime, scope,
judgment or outcome.
