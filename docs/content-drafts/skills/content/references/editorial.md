# Editorial judgment and reuse

Resolve the reader/viewer, their current problem or belief, desired shift,
one useful promise, available proof, voice, format and delivery intent. Ask
only for a material unresolved decision. Preserve uncertainty, qualifications,
source meaning and rights. Recording is an owner activity, not an automated
prerequisite. Keep working material at its actual owner, normally `content/`.

For substantial new content, an optional [brief](../assets/templates/video-brief.md)
can connect beats to evidence. For footage, use a [cut plan](../assets/templates/cut-plan.md)
for removals, preserved meaning, risky cuts and timing. Resolved conversation
inputs can already supply this; do not create duplicate records.

Choose structure for understanding: a concrete hook and promise, proof, a
useful explanation or plan, and a contextual next step. Three points can help,
but are not a fixed form. Draw ideas from work/client questions, mistakes,
mechanisms, cases and a supported point of view. Never manufacture the author’s
experience or claims. Use the shared human-writing method linked by Content.

For capture planning, the optional [format library](../assets/formats.json)
provides nine forms across teach/explain, prove/demonstrate, converse/learn
and humanize/relate. Choose for the evidence available and owner capacity.
Attention, nurture/proof and conversion describe intended jobs, not a mandated
funnel. Its cadence and minority-vlog figures are historical planning examples;
accept sustainable defaults with the owner. [Research provenance](research-provenance.md)
separates attributed creator claims, caption observations and product choices.

Reuse the core idea deliberately. Inspect the source/master and its exact
review, select a useful excerpt or adaptation, and reshape the hook, depth,
format, visual proof and CTA for its audience. A short must make sense outside
the long-form context. A newsletter or post needs its own coherent argument;
one video file or copy should not be duplicated blindly across channels.
Create only useful requested derivatives, using [lineage](graph.md) when needed.
Every derivative retains source meaning and its own review; master approval
never substitutes for derivative acceptance.

For visual direction, use accepted guidance at the actual design owner.
Content and Diffusion work without a design handoff. When supplied, receive
only an immutable `DESIGN.md` snapshot and selected assets, and record revision,
paths, hashes, provenance and review reference in the optional graph field.
Do not create an editable-source bridge or a design runtime dependency.
New reusable direction remains the design owner’s responsibility.

Inspect the finished artifact with [final review](final-review.md). Rework the
content as well as its wording if it fails the promise, evidence or intended
use. For a material decision, compare reasonable alternatives against actual
constraints and record rationale, consequences and supersession near the work.
Production-specific decisions can stay in existing notes or this optional
[production record](../assets/templates/project.md); no recovery ledger is needed.
