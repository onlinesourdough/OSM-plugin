# Final artifact and packaging review

Review the actual candidate against the requested outcome, relevant source,
brief/cut plan, transcript, accepted style/design and packaging. Load only inputs
that affect this artifact. A short piece gets a direct editorial read; a video
requires watching/listening to the export, not just its timeline or screenshots.
Use the [review template](../assets/templates/final-review.md) when a durable
node review is needed; existing review records can serve the same purpose.

Assess hook/promise, pacing, clarity, visual fit, audio/captions, packaging fit
and truthfulness. Mark inapplicable dimensions rather than inventing scores.
Check audible joins, context lost through cuts, caption accuracy/synchronization,
phone-size legibility, framing, proof and source rights where relevant. Record
timestamped/location-specific findings, severity, required fixes and evidence
limits. Missing transcript means spoken claims remain unverified; raw ASR is
not a reviewed quotation. Review each short, text adaptation and export on its
own terms, including cross-format consistency and a useful standalone payoff.

Packaging includes title, thumbnail/frame, description/copy, CTA and captions.
Use the optional [packaging review](../assets/templates/packaging-review.md) to
compare alternatives for clarity, curiosity, specificity, visual proof, channel
fit and mobile readability. Confirm the title and visual promise match the
final bytes; avoid unsupported clickbait or irrelevant CTAs.

A node becomes `approved` only when its designated reviewer accepts its exact
version and hash and records a review reference. Family, thesis, source, master
or sibling approval gives no inherited approval. An agent’s advisory score is
not a human decision. If bytes or meaning change, increment the affected
version, refresh the hash and obtain its own fresh review. Unchanged approved
nodes do not need a ritual reapproval. Fix in-scope findings and rerun affected
checks; state a missing designated review precisely without inventing acceptance.

Run [graph/handoff validation](graph.md) when used. Final review confers no
publishing authority. Report the candidate, evidence, outstanding decision and
useful next action in the existing record.
