Notion · Data & Context

The root starts with your business’s Data & Context definition: which systems to use, how to reach them, and what may be processed or stored where. The databases follow below.

Knowledge

Your business, offers, customers, voice and current understanding.

Memory

Lasting decisions, corrections and the reasons behind them.

Skills

Your own reusable ways of working.

Content · optional A database or links for your content work, when useful. Your definition can also point to other databases and systems.

Data & connected sources

Numina, CRM, files, messages and other systems keep their originals. Query them directly through approved tools. Source-only records stay there; a Notion mirror or importer is unnecessary.

Dreaming

Between sessions, review completed, unfinished and archived work. Recover missed learning, consolidate Memory, maintain the definition and repair verified mistakes without duplicating records.

Work + selected sources → review → maintained context

Setup configures the definition for your business. Access, permitted processing and retention are separate source rules. A daily Dreaming run uses a schedule selected in the host.
