OSM helps work move from an understood need to a useful, checked result.

## Use the depth the work needs

A small question or text correction can stay small. Substantive work benefits from a clear outcome, relevant sources, a plan where useful, implementation and review.

| Part | Useful question |
| --- | --- |
| Understand | What is the person trying to achieve, and what is still materially unclear? |
| Define | What result is accepted, what evidence will assess it, and where does the work belong? |
| Do | What needs to be created, changed or investigated? |
| Review | Does the actual result meet the accepted outcome? |
| Deliver | Has the selected destination received the reviewed result under the applicable authority? |
| Learn | Which useful decision or correction should remain findable next time? |

Reuse decisions and evidence already present. Do not make the owner repeat discovery or force every task through a full ceremony.

## Judge the actual result

The proof depends on the work. Read the final text in its intended format. See and hear an exported video. Exercise an application interaction. Reconcile a report against its sources. A valid file or successful tool call alone may not prove the result is useful.

Draft, ready, scheduled and actually delivered are different states. An uncertain send or write should be checked before retrying.

## Write for the reader

Lead with the outcome, finding or decision, in language the reader can use on a first reading. Keep important uncertainty and limitations visible. Put deeper evidence where the destination normally keeps it; include a session or process log only when it belongs there.

For an authorised Notion page, reuse its existing structure, template, properties and useful length. Relevant permitted examples can help establish that format. A short decision stays short; an established long guide may stay long. This applies to pages outside the context home too. Formatting creates no new access, retention or write permission.

## Evaluate what the goal calls for

For content, views can help diagnose distribution. They do not by themselves establish audience fit, trust or commercial value.

Keep concrete observations with the actual edition and measurement window. Use them to choose a bounded next experiment; avoid causal claims from a single post.

## Choose specialists when needed

Use an appropriate specialist or separately maintained system for the accepted work. Agentic Systems can support engineering projects. The optional Content companion can support editorial production. Neither needs to replace the owner's own process.
