OSM helps founders and small teams give their AI useful business data and context. It is a method for setting up that foundation, putting it to work and keeping it useful. The reference setup uses Notion as a shared workspace.

## Two parts of one foundation

Data is the material: records, documents, messages, recordings and evidence in the systems where they live.

Context gives it meaning and direction: goals, customers, offers, decisions, relationships, voice, constraints and working preferences. Skills belong inside context; they describe reusable ways of carrying out work.

Knowledge, Memory, Skills and an optional Content collection are storage responsibilities. They organize data and interpreted context; they do not add more conceptual pillars.

## What each place owns

| Place | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Shared context home | Maintained knowledge, goals, interpreted decisions, selected configuration and reusable methods. |
| Operational systems | Actual customer, financial, project and other live records. |
| Project or production workspace | Relevant sources, accepted scope, working files and reviewed results. |
| AI app | Models, tools, access, execution environment and native scheduling. |

Notion is the supported context home. Setup reuses suitable existing pages and databases. A route identifies a source; the selected account or connection supplies access.

## Shared method, personal configuration

OSM helps establish and maintain the foundation and apply the methods needed for the task. Your goals, taste, tools, voice and working rhythm remain your own context.

Content helps you build personal editorial methods on the foundation resolved by core Setup. It reuses the selected Content route; an existing human or assisted workflow can stay. Channels, formats, voice and production effort are adapted to your needs.

## Current explanation and history

Maintain one coherent current topic for each scope. Accepted corrections revise its explanation and Summary in place. Keep useful previous decisions and reasons in clearly marked history, reached when needed. Create another topic when it has an independent purpose or lifecycle.

Select relevant material by scope and task, accepted authority and comparable source freshness. Technical last-edited time is not confirmation that a fact is current.

## Your own AI operating model

Your AI operating model is the whole working arrangement: goals, people and AI, tools, methods, access and responsibilities. Its Data and Context Definition is a compact route and authority map inside that arrangement, not another name for the whole method. A structured block such as YAML helps people and AI read the map consistently; it is neither executable permission enforcement nor a promise of automatic caching.

Your own AI operating model home holds your company's selected configuration and knowledge. Setup adapts the shared template to that home.
