# Build your own content setup

OSM Content is an optional operating model beside OSM-plugin: general requirements, best practices, templates and skill skeletons. Use it to turn real work, ideas and references into useful content. You choose the channels, creative process and sustainable effort. The module provides a backbone for your setup.

Notion is the default runtime. Existing editors and external originals can stay; the same responsibilities work with equivalent records and review evidence outside Notion. Installing the plugin or reading this guide starts no content workflow.

## Start with your existing OSM records

OSM-plugin owns the context home, Knowledge/Memory/Skills routes and the selected Content binding with its minimum schema. This module adds optional editorial methods; it does not create a second profile, brand or priorities system. Reuse an existing Content destination or external workflow and respect a recorded deferral.

Find a few useful kinds of base records. Short names below are inspiration for finding their existing owners, not a required page list:

| Kind | Simple name inspiration | What Content uses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner and audience | About / Soul | Profile, brand, audience and point of view |
| Direction | Vision | Goals, priorities, offers and channel strategy |
| Expression and boundaries | Voice / Policy | Voice, operating rules and review authority |
| Working choices | Plan | Selected cadence and formats |
| Production building blocks | Templates | Reusable production templates and material |

Keep each fact at its existing owner. Knowledge owns lasting business direction and working preferences; Content owns production material and feedback. Personal Skills carry working steps. Memory keeps useful lasting decisions and needs few or no foundation files. Create no empty mandatory profile, strategy or department pages.

A best-practice <b>Foundation</b> view in Knowledge and Content shows the base records the module and agent get most value from, so useful guidance is in place before ideas and production accumulate. Filter actual base records using suitable existing metadata or explicit selection, excluding Superseded records. Link across existing owners rather than duplicating pages or moving them to fill a view.

Knowledge status <b>Current</b> means selected for use; <b>Needs review</b> means useful uncertainty, never age alone; <b>Superseded</b> means replaced and retained as history. Historical temporal scope is separate. Current and All are the normal views; an optional Attention view, sometimes called <b>Needs attention</b>, is a navigation aid, not a topic or niche. Content production states have their own configured meanings.

## Use two small skill axes

| Axis | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Responsibility skills | Setup, Intake, Prepare, Produce / Distribute and Learn cover the life of the work |
| Channel working methods (arbejdsformer) | One small guideline per active channel explains how to assemble a piece that fits the channel, your vision and its goal |

Existing skills may already cover responsibilities. Adapt only what helps. The primary <mention url="https://app.notion.com/p/4226d2e17f568142a957f3c3577e106b">content-channel</mention> is read and replicated by an agent for your selected channel or format. Newsletter, LinkedIn, X and YouTube are existing thin example copies, not a growing catalogue to install. Each working method links to your context, explains assembly and hands the result to the shared quality-gate skill. The optional newsletter-to-posts helper coordinates reuse when you select it; no channel is mandatory upstream.

## Build only the setup you need

1. Inspect your existing base records, Content route, tools and useful methods. Resolve only gaps affecting the work you selected.
2. Adapt one channel guideline with pointers to your vision, channel goal, audience, voice, policy, format and actual material. Keep personal facts and choices in your own setup.
3. Make a real draft in a non-publishing state. One production record may cover one or more channels; keep one editable original per independently maintained version and clear review evidence per destination.
4. Run the quality gate before asking you to review. Apply corrections, resolve addressed comments and check that the whole piece makes sense. Unresolved issues hold that handoff. In Notion, write draft prose you can comment on directly.
5. You mark approval of the final version and destinations. A gate pass or Ready does not approve publication. Delivery also needs the actual authority you gave and a verified result. Changed material needs renewed approval.
6. Keep feedback with the work and update a lasting choice once at its existing owner. Retain replaced guidance as Superseded history rather than deleting it.

A schedule helps make workload and upcoming review visible. The [schedule skeleton](https://github.com/onlinesourdough/OSM-Content/blob/5e5da443a67f68925c8b269d64e6c4b693a934e4/templates/content-schedule/SKILL.md) reads your cadence entry in Knowledge or its equivalent and proposes a plan. You choose the rhythm, optional horizon and review route; no posting days or volume are prescribed. A plan creates no scheduler job.

An optional best practice from the first real run is a short approval window for the next few days, with review ping-pong: the assistant prepares and checks, you comment, the assistant revises and resolves addressed comments, then you review again and mark final approval. Choose the rhythm yourself; no minutes, named statuses or posting times are prescribed. A bank replenished on your chosen cycle and owner-defined value-category caps can also help. These are not prerequisites for a draft.

## One small example

A bicycle workshop wants an occasional LinkedIn post explaining a useful maintenance check. Its existing About and Audience records describe the business and customers; Voice and Policy already govern language and review. Core OSM has prepared the selected Content destination. Content reuses those records.

- <b>Intake:</b> save the owner's permitted observation and why a rider would care.
- <b>Prepare:</b> check the advice, choose a real photo with permission and identify one point.
- <b>Produce / Distribute:</b> adapt one content-channel method: show the symptom, explain the check and end with a useful next action. Run the shared quality gate; ask the owner to review the editable draft. Publish only the approved version and destination under explicit delivery authority.
- <b>Learn:</b> keep a useful correction with the piece. If it changes a lasting format choice, update that choice at its existing owner.

These responsibilities are a loop, not a compulsory sequence of five skills. One method may cover several of them. If the owner later wants a rhythm, use the schedule reference in content-channel's Files; a proposed calendar creates no automation.

## Practical starting points

For one piece, use the [short-post workflow](https://github.com/onlinesourdough/OSM-Content/blob/5e5da443a67f68925c8b269d64e6c4b693a934e4/docs/post-workflow.md). Learn from selected evidence and exact corrections with the [writing-voice workflow](https://github.com/onlinesourdough/OSM-Content/blob/5e5da443a67f68925c8b269d64e6c4b693a934e4/docs/writing-voice.md). Keep a concrete reason and relevant timestamp for creative references using the [reference workflow](https://github.com/onlinesourdough/OSM-Content/blob/5e5da443a67f68925c8b269d64e6c4b693a934e4/docs/reference-library.md).

Give your agent your private context-home link and a selected task. Ask it to reuse existing records, read relevant policy, preserve your originals and prepare a reviewable result. A complete setup interview or style calibration is not required for every request. A saved skill alone does not prove native adoption or an editor's capabilities.

[Shared source](https://github.com/onlinesourdough/OSM-Content) and [published releases](https://github.com/onlinesourdough/OSM-Content/releases) identify what is available. This repository is outside OSM-plugin. Personal content, media, configuration and adapted skills stay in your selected private home. The method grants no publication or scheduling authority.
